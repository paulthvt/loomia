# Undo a tick — plan

**Goal:** every tick offers Undo for 5 s and takes back all it wrote (#372).
**Architecture:** the tick writes at once as before; Undo calls one reverse RPC
per kind, guarded by the person's `updated_at`. One helper,
`lib/features/contacts/presentation/undo_tick.dart`, shows the snackbar and
runs the undo on the app's provider container.
**Tech Stack:** Flutter, Riverpod, Supabase (plpgsql, pgTAP).
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-undo-tick-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Additive migration only; the released app never calls the new functions.
- No colour, width or duration literal in a widget except the named
  constants in `undo_tick.dart`.

## Rulings made while planning

- `Person.updatedAt` is a `String`, exactly as PostgREST sends it: a web
  `DateTime` keeps milliseconds only, and the guard compares microseconds.
- A failure snackbar (`writePeople`, Today's event step) replaces an Undo
  still showing instead of queueing 5 s behind it.
- The workflow timeline and the contact page share `tickStep` /
  `tickReminder`; Today keeps its own `_tick` because it also counts the tick.
- After an undo on Today, `doneTodayProvider` is invalidated and counts again.
- `material_ui` 1.5.0 (the version locked here) defaults `persist` to on with
  an action, as the review found in 1.8.0.

## Review Focus

- `undo_step` is `security definer`: its owner check on person and step.
- The entry an undo deletes: `created_at = p_updated_at` for a step or LRP
  check, latest matching text for a reminder.
