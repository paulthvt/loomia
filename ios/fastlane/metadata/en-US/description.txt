Loomia is for people whose business runs on relationships: the customers you look after, the people curious about what you do, and the team you work with. It keeps them all in one calm place and answers one question each morning: who should I get back to today?

TODAY
The people worth a message today, your tasks and today's events, on one screen. Mark a follow-up as done, plan it for later, or log what happened in a few taps. Nothing turns red, nothing nags.

CONTACTS
Each person shows where they are and what comes next: the step of their workflow, your notes, their tags and everything that happened between you. Import people from your phone's contacts, only the ones you pick.

WORKFLOWS
Write down what you usually do with someone, step by step: send samples, check in after a week, ask how it went. Loomia brings the next step to Today when it's due.

CALENDAR
Plan a workshop or a product evening, invite people, then note who was there. Everyone who came moves on to the right follow-up, without retyping anything.

MONTH PLAN
Pick a few goals for the month and see how it's going, at your own pace. You compare yourself with your own plan, never with anyone else.

YOUR DATA
No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. Everything is stored in the European Union, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

One account on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. In English and French.
